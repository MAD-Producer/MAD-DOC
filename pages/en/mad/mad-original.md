## MAD

**MAD**(MAD Movie<span class="small">／</span>マッドムービー）；（English：Videos of Madness）<sup>[\[1\]](https://zh.wikipedia.org/wiki/MAD%E7%89%87#cite_note-1)</sup>refers to the act of taking existing[sound](https://zh.wikipedia.org/wiki/%E5%A3%B0%E9%9F%B3),[game](https://zh.wikipedia.org/wiki/%E7%94%B5%E5%AD%90%E6%B8%B8%E6%88%8F),[image](https://zh.wikipedia.org/wiki/%E5%9B%BE%E5%83%8F),[animation](https://zh.wikipedia.org/wiki/%E5%8A%A8%E7%94%BB)and other elements that are edited and compiled by individuals to create a new work.[game](https://zh.wikipedia.org/wiki/%E6%B8%B8%E6%88%8F)culture,[animation](https://zh.wikipedia.org/wiki/%E5%8A%A8%E7%94%BB)culture,[fanworks](https://zh.wikipedia.org/wiki/%E5%90%8C%E4%BA%BA_(ACG))within the cultural categories[multimedia](https://zh.wikipedia.org/wiki/%E5%A4%9A%E5%AA%92%E9%AB%94)works. Typically, a work consists of a segment of related video.[editing](https://zh.wikipedia.org/wiki/%E5%89%AA%E8%BC%AF), accompanied by the author's favorite music, similar to[fan-made MV](https://zh.wikipedia.org/wiki/%E5%8A%A8%E7%94%BB%E9%9F%B3%E4%B9%90%E5%BD%95%E5%83%8F)the existence of this form. The key difference is that MAD videos focus on the visuals, particularly related to animation or video games.[tribute](https://zh.wikipedia.org/wiki/%E8%87%B4%E6%95%AC); in contrast, fan-made MVs focus more on the music. Of course, there are also some MAD videos that consist solely of audio editing or visual editing, which can strictly be considered as MAD as well.

## Origin of MAD

<span style="color: #202122;font-family: sans-serif;font-size: 15.008px">The history of the term "MADMOVIE" begins with MADtape: around 1978</span><sup><a href="https://zh.wikipedia.org/wiki/MAD#cite_note-2" style="color: var(--color-progressive,#36c);background: none" target="_self">[2]</a></sup><span style="color: #202122;font-family: sans-serif;font-size: 15.008px">, "Mr. MAD Shimakawa" and "Mr. Y," founding members of a club at Osaka University of Arts, while playing anime songs or</span><a href="https://zh.wikipedia.org/wiki/" style="font-family: sans-serif;font-size: 15.008px;background: none;color: var(--color-progressive,#36c)" target="_self" title="guitar">guitar</a><span style="color: #202122;font-family: sans-serif;font-size: 15.008px"> for</span><a href="https://zh.wikipedia.org/wiki/" style="font-family: sans-serif;font-size: 15.008px;background: none;color: var(--color-progressive,#36c)" target="_self" title="special effects (often refers to tokusatsu)">tokusatsu</a><span style="color: #202122;font-family: sans-serif;font-size: 15.008px">, tried playing music with a different melody than the main theme (for example, playing a melody different from the original arrangement during the verse, and then perfectly matching the original melody when transitioning to the chorus, and so on, with each section of the song being treated in this way). This resulted in a somewhat hilarious and fun effect, and before they knew it, this way of playing became the norm in the club.</span>

## The Rise and Fall of the MAD Community

Several factors influence the number of MAD creators (referring to Japan):

- 
- With the development of computer technology, the production of <a href="https://zh.wikipedia.org/wiki/Flash" target="_self">Flash</a> short films became easier, the file sizes were smaller, and the circulation greatly increased.

<!-- -->

- - In the early years, many MAD creators were invited by video game companies or animation studios to become professional producers.

<!-- -->

- - Many video game titles produced promotional shorts similar to MAD videos, paired with the game's theme song and made available for interested users to download on the game's official website. Notably, many of these promotional shorts were created by early MAD producers.

<!-- -->

- - The general trend of younger demographics using home computers, coupled with a decrease in individuals possessing significant computer skills who are also passionate about \*nijigen\* (2D) culture, has led to a talent gap in production and a lack of new, younger creators.<sup><a href="https://zh.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wikipedia:" target="_self">[Citation needed]</a></sup>

<!-- -->

- - Due to the popularity of video-sharing websites such as <a href="https://zh.wikipedia.org/wiki/NICONICO" target="_self">NICONICO Douga</a> and <a href="https://zh.wikipedia.org/wiki/YouTube" target="_self">YouTube</a>, MAD videos have become more diverse and accessible to a wider audience.
