## About This Site

MAD DOC is written mainly for mainland Chinese MAD·AMV creators who publish, compete, and connect on **Bilibili**. Material on Japanese MAD and Western AMV explains origins, terminology, and mutual influence.

Principles:

- **Primary sources first**: creators' original posts, contest pages, and official software documentation take priority over reposts.
- **Facts and opinions kept apart**: every historical claim is labeled for reliability, and platform rules, community habits, and personal views are distinguished.
- **Differing accounts preserved**: there is no single worldwide classification of MAD and AMV, so different views are recorded side by side.
- **No infringing resources**: pages explain lawful ways to obtain material and do not list source download sites or cracked software.

Time-sensitive pages such as the contest guidelines, AI assistance, and the source index state their verification date at the top. Software, platform rules, and contest details change quickly; check the latest official versions before relying on them.
