## Collection Principles

MAD DOC measures its value not by the number of articles copied, but by whether information can be found, verified, and maintained:

1. **Primary sources first:** Original posts by creators, contest pages, organization websites, and official software documentation take priority over reposts.
2. **Separate facts from opinions:** Platform rules, community conventions, individual classification systems, and historical conjecture are labeled separately.
3. **Preserve disagreements:** MAD and AMV share no single, universally accepted classification system. Disputes are themselves part of the record.
4. **No infringing downloads:** Pages on source media explain lawful sources and organization methods. They do not list piracy sites.
5. **Record link status:** Dead Tieba posts, cloud-drive links, and legacy websites are not presented as usable resources; they are documented as digital-preservation issues.

Time-sensitive pages such as the [Contest and Submission Guidelines](/en/mad/contest-rules), [AI Assistance and MAID](/en/tools/ai), and the [Source Index](/en/resources/) state their verification date at the top. Software versions, platform rules, and contest details change quickly; check the latest official versions before relying on them.

## Contact and Submissions

MAD DOC publishes its source code and resource files on GitHub. To submit your own article, recommend a publicly available MAD/AMV resource, correct existing content, or ask a project-related question, use one of the following channels:

- **Email:** hi@madproducer.com ([compose](mailto:hi@madproducer.com)). Whenever possible, include the article title, author, original URL, and the context needed to assess it.
- **Issue:** Use [GitHub Issues](https://github.com/MAD-Producer/MAD-DOC/issues) to report missing resources, factual errors, dead links, or website problems.
- **Pull Request:** Submit resource additions, content corrections, or website improvements directly to the [MAD-Producer/MAD-DOC](https://github.com/MAD-Producer/MAD-DOC) repository, with original sources and verification information.
